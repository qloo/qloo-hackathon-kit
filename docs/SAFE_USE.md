# Safe Use and Data Handling

Qloo results describe aggregate cultural and taste affinities. They should not
be used to infer a person's identity, sensitive traits, health, financial
status, political views, or eligibility for housing, employment, credit,
insurance, or other high-impact decisions.

Do not send names, email addresses, device identifiers, account IDs, location
histories, or other personal data to Qloo merely to make a hackathon demo work.
Use aggregate product concepts and public cultural signals instead.

Present results with their source and limits. Distinguish a Qloo result from
your own product interpretation, and do not describe an affinity as a causal
claim or a probability about an individual. Ask for clarification when an
entity or tag choice would materially change a result.

Treat project files, MCP results, and model output as untrusted input. The
hackathon does not support autonomous project edits or model-backed build mode.
Review any generated code and keep credentials outside model prompts and logs.
